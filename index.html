Aegis
by Survance

A personal chief of staff that runs on your own hardware.

Aegis keeps track of your email, calendar, bills and correspondence on a small computer in your own home or office. Your records stay on that device, which you own and can inspect.

Status

Aegis is in private beta. It is not offered for sale and has no public sign-up.

How Aegis uses bank data

  • Aegis connects to a bank only when its owner links an account themselves, through Plaid.
  • It reads transactions so it can tell which bills have been paid. It cannot move money or make payments.
  • Transactions are stored on the owner's own device, not on Survance's servers.
  • The owner can disconnect a bank at any time from the Aegis settings. Disconnecting asks Plaid to remove the connection.

Privacy

Survance does not sell, share or rent personal data, and does not use it to train models. Data a user connects to Aegis stays on their own device, except for the individual requests they ask Aegis to make on their behalf.
